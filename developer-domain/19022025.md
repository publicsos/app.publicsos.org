Okay, here's the updated list of roles with the Mental Health Coordinator and Spiritual Care Coordinator added, along with some refinements and clarifications based on our discussion:

| Role Name                     | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Incident Commander            | Has full control over the system, can manage all users and settings. Makes ultimate decisions regarding resource allocation and strategic response.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Operations Chief              | Manages rescue operations, assigns teams, and monitors field response. Coordinates with other chiefs to ensure effective deployment of resources.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Medical Chief                 | Oversees all medical aspects of the crisis response. Assigns and manages paramedics, medical teams, and field hospitals. Coordinates with hospitals and other medical facilities.                                                                                                                              |
| Fire & HAZMAT Chief          | Controls firefighting and hazardous materials response teams.  Develops strategies for containing and mitigating fires and hazardous material spills.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Engineering Chief             | Manages structural assessments and infrastructure repair teams.  Prioritizes repairs to critical infrastructure, such as bridges, roads, and utilities.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Law Enforcement Chief         | Handles security, police, and military coordination.  Ensures the safety and security of responders and the public. Manages crowd control and evacuation procedures.                                                                                                                                     |
| Logistics Coordinator         | Manages emergency supplies, fuel, and transportation.  Ensures that responders have the resources they need.  Coordinates the delivery of supplies to affected areas.                                                                                                                                   |
| Communication Officer         | Controls emergency broadcasts, GIS, and public updates.  Manages communication with the media and the public.  Disseminates timely and accurate information.                                                                                                                                              |
| Shelter Coordinator           | Manages evacuation centers, food distribution, and survivor care.  Ensures that evacuees have access to shelter, food, and other essential services.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Field Responder               | Has limited access to update reports, mark areas as cleared, and report new incidents.  Provides real-time information from the field. Can upload photos and other relevant data.                                                                                                                      |
| Mental Health Coordinator     | Oversees mental health services. Coordinates psychologists, counselors, and other mental health professionals. Establishes psychological first aid and trauma-informed care protocols. Manages mental health resources and referrals.                                                                      |
| Spiritual Care Coordinator    | Facilitates spiritual support for those affected by the crisis. Coordinates chaplains, priests, imams, rabbis, and other religious leaders. Establishes partnerships with faith-based organizations. Manages requests for spiritual care and support.                                                     |
| Planning Officer              | Develops and maintains crisis plans, conducts risk assessments, and organizes training exercises.  Works with other roles to ensure preparedness for a variety of crises.                                                                                                                            |
| Intelligence Analyst          | Gathers and analyzes information to anticipate potential crises and provide situational awareness.  Monitors potential threats and provides early warnings.                                                                                                                                             |
| Recovery Manager              | Oversees the long-term recovery efforts, including infrastructure rebuilding, economic revitalization, and community support.  Develops and implements recovery plans.                                                                                                                                    |
| Data Manager                  | Manages the CRM data, ensures data integrity, and generates reports.  Provides data analysis to support decision-making.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Public Information Officer (PIO) | Handles communication with the media and the public.  Develops and disseminates press releases.  Manages social media communications.  Ensures consistent and accurate messaging.  May be combined with Communication Officer in smaller organizations.                                                    |
| Cybersecurity Chief            | Manages cybersecurity during a crisis.  Protects critical systems and data from cyberattacks.  Develops and implements cybersecurity protocols.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Volunteer Coordinator           | Manages volunteers who offer assistance during a crisis.  Recruits, trains, and deploys volunteers.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Donations Manager              | Handles the receipt and distribution of donations.  Ensures that donations are used effectively and efficiently.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Administrative Support         | Provides administrative assistance to the crisis management team.  Manages logistics, scheduling, and other administrative tasks.                                                                                                                                                                       |
