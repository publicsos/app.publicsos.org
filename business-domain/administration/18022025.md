**Subject:** Implementation of Board Structure and Decision-Making Procedures

---

**Proposal:**
This motion seeks to establish a structured framework for board governance, ensuring secure, accurate, and efficient decision-making processes. The adoption of a formal board structure will enhance transparency, accountability, and organizational efficiency.

---

**Board Voting Procedures:**
To maintain a clear and systematic approach to board decision-making, the following procedures are proposed:

1. **Motion Introduction**  
   - A motion is a formal proposal for action or resolution presented by a board director.
   - If introduced before a meeting, the director submits the motion to the board chair, who includes it in the agenda.
   - If introduced during a meeting, the director presents it as an additional agenda item for consideration.

2. **Seconding the Motion**  
   - A motion requires a second from another director to proceed.
   - Seconding does not indicate agreement but signifies that the motion warrants discussion.
   - If no second is received, the motion may be amended for future discussion or declared void.

3. **Discussion**  
   - Upon being seconded, the board chair opens the floor for discussion.
   - Each board member is given an equal opportunity to provide input.
   - The chair ensures orderly discussion and facilitates a fair debate.

4. **Voting Process**  
   - After discussions, the chair calls for a vote.
   - Prior to voting, directors may choose to amend, withdraw, or proceed with the motion.
   - Voting options include: **Yes**, **No**, or **Abstain**.
   - The final tally is recorded and announced.
   - If all directors vote **Yes**, the motion is approved through unanimous written consent.

---

**Implementation & Compliance:**
- The proposed board structure will be documented and incorporated into the organization’s governance framework.
- Board members will undergo an orientation session to ensure full comprehension of the new procedures.
- A periodic review will be conducted to assess the effectiveness and adherence to these governance principles.

**Conclusion:**
The adoption of this motion will strengthen the organization's decision-making process, ensuring a more transparent, structured, and effective governance framework. We recommend its immediate implementation to streamline board operations and enhance accountability.
