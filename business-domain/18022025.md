What is the EIC Accelerator Open
The EIC Accelerator Open calls have no predefined topic: This call is for innovations in any field of technology and innovations that cut across different scientific, technological, sectoral and application fields.

Short applications: any time (continuous)

Full application deadlines: 12 March 2025, 1 October 2025

Check the main EIC Accelerator page for details on eligiblity, deadlines and applications process.
